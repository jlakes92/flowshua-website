About Flowshua

Technical operations. Program execution discipline.

Joshua Lakes, PMP, is a U.S. Navy aviation veteran whose background includes coordinating personnel, schedules, technical requirements, material readiness, quality, documentation, and operational execution in a highly regulated environment. He later formalized that experience through project-management education and PMP certification.

Flowshua applies that disciplined foundation to broader technical project and program support, including coordination, readiness visibility, quality and compliance support, project controls, and technical documentation. This experience does not represent aircraft maintenance authority, completed corporate aviation maintenance work, or government contract past performance.

  • PMP-certified owner
  • Six years of U.S. Navy aviation maintenance, readiness, and quality-assurance experience
  • CDI, CDQAR, ACSP Entry Authority, E-2/C-2 APU/LPT, and NALCOMIS experience
